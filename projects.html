Research

PaCX-MAE

Contributed to a cross-modal self-supervised learning framework that distills physiological signals (ECG and lab data) into a unimodal chest X-ray encoder.

Experimented with distillation and led dataset preprocessing & evaluation on clinical benchmarks (MedMod + CheXchoNet), including systematic component studies (low-data efficiency and ablations) to isolate model behavior.

Accepted as a poster presentation at the 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 3rd Annual Workshop on Multi-modal Foundation Models and Large Language Models for Life Sciences (FM4LS).

Tools: PyTorch, PyTorch Lightning, WandB, DDP, Slurm, LoRA, Scikit-learn

GraphiStasis

Used MLP and GNNs (SAGE, GCN, GAT) to predict epistatic interactions using a bipartite gene-disease graph.

Tested the models' viability on a set of 108 schizophrenia genes identified by a 2014 GWAS and evaluated embedding space validity using PCA.

Currently exploring follow-up analyses and different gene embeddings.

Tools: PyTorch Geometric, PyTorch, Biopython Entrez, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib

IE Risk Modeling

Developed and compared predictive models (XG Boost, Random Forest, Logistic Regression) for infective endocarditis prediction from triage data using the MIMIC-IV dataset.

Systematically explored hyperparameter tuning and chief complaint encoding strategies. Performed internal (MIMIC-IV) and external (MC-MED) validation.

Identified key risk factors and evaluated model performance using AUPRC and decision curve analysis.

Tools: Scikit-learn, Slurm, Bash, XGBoost, DataFrames, Matplotlib

AFib Risk Modeling

Developed and compared 2 predictive models (XG Boost and GLM) for atrial fibrillation diagnosis using the Synthea Adult Rhode Island dataset.
