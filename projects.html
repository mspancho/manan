Research

AI Agents in a Simulated ED

Conceived and led a study of AI agents replicating a randomized trial of a chest-pain decision rule in a simulated emergency department with auditable data-generating processes.

1st-author manuscript under review (2026): LLM-agent simulation of operational decisions in a clinical environment.

Research conducted at Harvard Medical School’s Department of Biomedical Informatics under Pranav Rajpurkar, PhD.

Research

PaCX-MAE

Contributed to a cross-modal self-supervised learning framework that distills physiological signals (ECG and lab data) into a unimodal chest X-ray encoder.

Co-developed the framework, which teaches chest X-ray models to draw on electrocardiogram and laboratory data during training; PaCX-MAE improves disease prediction in settings with limited training data. Brown University Department of Computer Science graduate seminar research project (Oct 2025 - Jul 2026).

Experimented with distillation and led dataset preprocessing & evaluation on clinical benchmarks (MedMod + CheXchoNet), including systematic component studies (low-data efficiency and ablations) to isolate model behavior.

Accepted as a poster presentation at the 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML) 3rd Annual Workshop on Multi-modal Foundation Models and Large Language Models for Life Sciences (FM4LS), Seoul, South Korea, July 2026.

Tools: PyTorch, PyTorch Lightning, WandB, DDP, Slurm, LoRA, Scikit-learn

Research

GraphiStasis

Used MLP and GNNs (SAGE, GCN, GAT) to predict epistatic interactions using a bipartite gene-disease graph.

Tested the models' viability on a set of 108 schizophrenia genes identified by a 2014 GWAS and evaluated embedding space validity using PCA.

Currently exploring follow-up analyses and different gene embeddings.

Tools: PyTorch Geometric, PyTorch, Biopython Entrez, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib

GLP-1 & Joint Replacement Risk

First-authored a two-sample multivariable Mendelian randomization study showing that genetically proxied GLP-1 receptor activation lowers osteoarthritis-related joint replacement risk independent of body weight.

Conducted with Jagat Chauhan, PhD at Apex Heart Institute. Manuscript under review (2026).
